Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

In short

Novely has no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no trackers. We do not track you across apps and websites and do not pass data to brokers. Your library is kept in your own iCloud storage, which the developer cannot access. The one thing that becomes visible to other people is the friends feed, and only if you turn it on yourself.

Who handles your data

The controller of the little data this app processes is Novely's developer: Demina Alina, 144010, Russia. Contact: app.zimin@gmail.com

What is stored, and where

Your library, shelves, progress, reading sessions, quotes, goals, name, username and profile photo are kept on the device and synchronised through the private database of your own iCloud storage — so that they survive a lost device and appear on a new one. Apple is the processor here, the same relationship Photos or Notes have with iCloud; the developer of Novely has no access to that database. Without an iCloud account the app works entirely locally. The friends feed is separate: if you turn it on, your name and the books you marked as read or rated go into the public iCloud database and become visible to the people you gave an invite link. It is off by default, and with it off nothing leaves your devices.

Network requests

The app calls the Google Books API and Open Library to find books and load covers. Those requests carry only your search text and technical headers. We do not send your name, reading statistics, quotes or device identifiers. Those services handle requests under their own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and archive.org/about/terms.php.

Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only to scan ISBNs, recognise quotes, and photograph a custom book cover. Images are processed on the device and are never sent anywhere. Photo access is used only to pick a profile picture, book cover, or page photo for quote recognition.

Notifications

Release notifications and reading reminders are scheduled locally on the device. The app has no push servers.

Children

Novely is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Search queries external book catalogues whose contents we do not control.

Your rights

Your data sits on your device and in your iCloud, so you can always reach and change it — right inside the app. You can export a copy in Settings, “Export data (.csv)”. Anything published to the friends feed is removed in the same place: Settings → Friends Feed → “Delete what was published earlier”. If your law grants you further rights (access, rectification, erasure, a complaint to a supervisory authority), write to us and we will help.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app erases the data on the device itself, but not what has already gone to iCloud. To remove that too: first turn the friends feed off in the app and tap “Delete what was published earlier”, or what you published stays visible to friends; then delete the app; then delete its iCloud data via iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Novely. Before deleting, it is worth exporting everything to CSV: Profile → Settings → Export data.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy and the date at the bottom of this screen before the change takes effect.
