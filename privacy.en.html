Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

In short

Novely does not collect or transmit your personal data. The app has no server, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no trackers. We do not track you across apps and websites, and we do not share data with brokers.

Who handles your data

The developer of Novely is the data controller for the little data the app handles. Contact: app.zimin@gmail.com

What is stored, and where

Your library, shelves, progress, reading sessions, quotes, goals, name, username and profile photo are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is enabled in the system, that data may end up in your own iCloud backup — which belongs to you, and which the developer cannot access.

Network requests

The app calls the Google Books API and Open Library to find books and load covers. Those requests carry only your search text and technical headers. We do not send your name, reading statistics, quotes or device identifiers. Those services handle requests under their own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and archive.org/about/terms.php.

Camera and photos

Camera access is requested only to scan ISBNs, recognise quotes, and photograph a custom book cover. Images are processed on the device and are never sent anywhere. Photo access is used only to pick a profile picture, book cover, or page photo for quote recognition.

Notifications

Release notifications and reading reminders are scheduled locally on the device. The app has no push servers.

Children

Novely is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data. Search queries external book catalogues whose contents we do not control.

Your rights

Because the data sits on your device, you can always see and change it — right in the app. Export a copy from Settings, “Export data (.csv)”; delete everything by deleting the app. If your law gives you further rights (access, rectification, erasure, or a complaint to a supervisory authority), write to us and we will help.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all local Novely data. You can export everything to CSV first: Profile → Settings → Export data.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this policy and the date at the bottom of this screen before the change takes effect.
